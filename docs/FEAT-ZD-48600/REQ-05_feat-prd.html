Product Requirement Document for FEAT-ZD-48600 (ZD-48600, "Show which overdue invoices belong to vendors that usually pay late"). This is a draft for the REQ-05 gate (PM).

This PRD carries forward the decisions in the approved BRD (feat.brd). It adds no scope. It turns the BRD scope (S-1 to S-7) and business rules (BR-1 to BR-16) into user stories, numbered acceptance criteria in EARS form and non-functional requirements, and ends with a validation check. Statements marked "agent judgement" are this agent's own reading; no input states them as fact. PRD-level assumptions (P-A) and questions (PQ) are new in this step and need confirmation at the gate.

1. Summary

ItemStatementSource
Problem"AP managers want to see which overdue invoices belong to vendors that usually pay late, so they can chase them first."feat.brd (section 1)
What the product will doBuild each vendor's payment-delay history from a new optional paid_date in the vendor feed, give each vendor a rule-based risk level (high, medium, low) from its predicted delay, list unpaid invoices more than 30 days past due with that risk level, and show the list as a table in the portal.feat.brd (sections 2, 4)
Flowfeed -> delays -> risk -> overdue list -> portal table, proven end to end.feat.brd (S-7, SM-1)
UsersAP managers. Finance teams benefit indirectly (agent judgement carried from the BRD).feat.brd (section 3)
Business meaningCarried from the BRD as an assumption: the risk level helps AP managers decide which overdue payables to act on first (B-A1, still open as OQ-B1).feat.brd (B-A1, OQ-B1)
Architecture rule"Keep every existing signature; add new functions." Existing behaviour changes only in two additive ways: the optional risk argument on build_explanation_request (CR-4) and feed ingestion accepting the optional paid_date (CR-1).feat.brd (section 4, SM-5)
Not in this changeFinance posting, authentication, tenancy and payment execution; acting on the risk level automatically; an aging export (R-AP-14); a change of LLM provider or model; using an LLM to set the risk level; the work of ZD-48501 and ZD-48211; the regulatory "Late" rule (REG-LPR-2026-4.2).feat.brd (OS-1 to OS-8)

3. Acceptance criteria (EARS)

Each criterion uses the EARS form "When <trigger>, the system shall <response>" (org.templates). "Days" are calendar days after the due date (BR-9, B-A2).

3.1 Vendor feed paid_date (ap_pipeline, S-1, US-2)

#Acceptance criterionTraces toSource
AC-1When a vendor feed row has an empty paid_date, the system shall accept the row and set Invoice.paid_date to None.BR-1, S-1feat.brd (BR-1, S-1); file:hobasa-agentic-sdlc/requirements/ZD-48600.md (org.data_model: "paid_date (date or None)")
AC-2When a vendor feed row that is marked paid has a paid_date that is a valid ISO date, the system shall map it to Invoice.paid_date. (A row that is unpaid but has a valid paid_date is not covered here; its handling is open as OQ-B6.)BR-1, S-1feat.brd (BR-1, S-1, OQ-B6)
AC-3When a vendor feed row has a paid_date that is not empty and is not a valid ISO date, the system shall reject the row with the reason 'paid_date'.BR-2, SM-4feat.brd (BR-2, SM-4); assumption P-A3

3.2 Payment-delay history (ap_pipeline, S-2, US-2)

#Acceptance criterionTraces toSource
AC-4When delays_by_vendor(records) is called, the system shall return {vendor: [delay_days, ...]} built only from records that are paid and have a paid_date, where delay_days = (paid_date - due_date) in days, with each vendor's values in feed order.BR-3, S-2feat.brd (BR-3, S-2)
AC-5When a record is marked paid but has no paid_date, the system shall leave it out of the delay history and shall not reject it.B-A5feat.brd (B-A5)
AC-6When a paid record's paid_date is before its due_date, the system shall keep the negative delay_days as computed, with no floor.B-A4feat.brd (B-A4)

3.3 Vendor risk level and explanation (ap_forecast, S-3, S-4, US-3, US-4)

#Acceptance criterionTraces toSource
AC-7When vendor_risk(history) is called and predict_delay_days(history) > 30, the system shall return 'high'.BR-4feat.brd (BR-4)
AC-8When vendor_risk(history) is called and predict_delay_days(history) is greater than 10 and not greater than 30 (including exactly 30), the system shall return 'medium'.BR-4, BR-5, SM-2feat.brd (BR-4, BR-5, SM-2)
AC-9When vendor_risk(history) is called and predict_delay_days(history) is not greater than 10 (including exactly 10), the system shall return 'low'.BR-4, BR-5, SM-2feat.brd (BR-4, BR-5, SM-2)
AC-10When vendor_risk computes a risk level, the system shall apply the rule on the predicted delay only and shall not call an LLM.BR-6, OS-5feat.brd (BR-6, OS-5)
AC-11When build_explanation_request is called with a risk argument, the system shall build a prompt that ends with ' Risk level: <risk>.'.BR-15, S-4feat.brd (BR-15, S-4)
AC-12When build_explanation_request is called without a risk argument, the system shall build the same prompt as today.B-A6, SM-5feat.brd (B-A6, SM-5)

3.4 Overdue list with risk (ap_aging, S-5, US-1)

#Acceptance criterionTraces toSource
AC-13When overdue_with_risk(invoices, as_of, risk_by_vendor) is called, the system shall include an invoice only when it is unpaid and more than 30 days past due as of as_of, counting calendar days after the due date.BR-7, BR-9, S-5feat.brd (BR-7, BR-9, S-5); assumption P-A1
AC-14When an unpaid invoice is exactly 30 days past due as of as_of, the system shall not include it in the overdue list.BR-7, SM-2feat.brd (BR-7, SM-2); record:memory (L-007)
AC-15When an invoice is paid, the system shall exclude it from the overdue list, whatever its days past due.BR-8feat.brd (BR-8); record:memory (D-2026-050)
AC-16When the overdue list is returned, the system shall give each row the keys id, vendor, days, amount and risk, and shall sort the rows by days descending, then by id.BR-10feat.brd (BR-10); assumption P-A2
AC-17When an invoice's vendor has no entry in risk_by_vendor, the system shall set that row's risk to 'unknown'.BR-11feat.brd (BR-11)
AC-18When the system sets a row's amount, it shall use the Invoice amount as a Decimal with 2 dp; when the list is passed from ap_aging to ap_portal as JSON, the system shall carry the amount as a string with two decimals and dates as ISO strings.BR-12, B-A7, SM-3feat.brd (BR-12, B-A7, SM-3)

3.5 Portal table (ap_portal, S-6, US-1)

#Acceptance criterionTraces toSource
AC-19When renderOverdueRisk(rows) is called with a non-empty list of rows, the system shall render <table class="overdue-risk"> with one <tr class="risk-RISK"> per row, showing id, vendor, days and amount to two decimals under the columns Invoice, Vendor, Days overdue and Amount.BR-13, BR-14, S-6feat.brd (BR-13, BR-14, S-6)
AC-20When a row is rendered, the system shall set its class to risk-high, risk-medium, risk-low or risk-unknown to match the row's risk value.BR-14feat.brd (BR-14)
AC-21When renderOverdueRisk(rows) is called with no rows, the system shall return an empty string.BR-13feat.brd (BR-13); file:hobasa-agentic-sdlc/requirements/ZD-48600.md (org.design_library: "render nothing when there are no rows")
